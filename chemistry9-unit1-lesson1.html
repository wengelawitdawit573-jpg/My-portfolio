📖 Introduction to Chemistry

What is Chemistry?

Chemistry is the branch of science that studies matter, its properties, composition, structure, and the changes that matter undergoes.

🌍 Why is Chemistry Important?

Chemistry helps us understand many things around us, including materials, food, medicines, agriculture, energy, and the environment.
